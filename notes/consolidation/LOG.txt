All games: Slithery seed 1000, live flags + CONS vs variants/v32. 8 games total.

DESIGN
- Status beacon: L>=CONS_BEACON_MIN(5) dragons with idle rays (no king broadcast, no king relay, never on
  PORTAL_PROBE_SOLO turns) send encodeKing layout (post-move pos, len, id, dir, round) under STATUS_SECRET on all
  4 rays from CONS_R-10, through sonarOut() (so SONAR_ENCRYPT applies). decodeKing(SECRET) rejects them.
  Idle dragons relay the longest fresh status they heard (CONS_RELAY_HOPS 2).
- Host choice (pickConsHost, each turn from CONS_R, L<=CONS_MAXLEN(5), not king, units>max(CONS_KEEP 8, reserve),
  not threatened): candidates = visible ally heads (visible segment count), kingsSeen, statusSeen (<=CONS_MEM 6
  rounds old), len >= max(CONS_HOST_MIN 5, L+CONS_GAP 3) and >= CONS_HOST_FRAC(.75) x longest known, within
  CONS_RANGE 24; minimise dist - 0.5*len.
- Consolidating dragon: no growth splits (CONS_NOSPLIT; also would-be hosts L>=5 after CONS_R), no PF/HOT pulls,
  pull CONS_PULL 2.0/step to the host's 2-ahead tile. Throttle: if the host is in view and we are not first
  in line (closest SMALL ally head to the host), no pull inside 5 and -6 for tiles within 2 of the host head.
- Feed (tryConsFeed): host in view, dk<=3, on a1/a2/sides or within 2 of a2 and not behind (CONS_LOOSE), no enemy
  head within 4 of the host, previous meal eaten, no other small ally head as close to our head as the host
  (CONS_STEAL). CONS_OVER_KING 1: replaces the KING_V2 feed for dragons that have a host.

CONCLUSIONS
- Precision works: a CONS feed gives the host ~60% of its pearls (v32's loose king feed ~15%; the rest is
  eaten by the crowd of feeders, which then regrow to L4 and split 2+2 again).
- Unit count does NOT fall: cons keeps 30-45 units at r450-500 vs v32 4-26. Stricter feeding = fewer deaths,
  and dragons without a king-class host (most of them) keep the 4->2+2 split churn (~40-60 splits/25 rounds).
- Co-kings: t5 B ended 33/33 - two king-class dragons both fed. KING_CONSENSUS/merge (PLAN C) is the missing half.
- Not ready as a win-rate bet. If tested on the server: bot + live flags + -DCONS_R=330 (defaults above) vs v32,
  Slithery+Schooltime+Portals; expect ~even. Next ideas: CONS_HOST_FRAC .95 (only the single longest), pair with
  KC_STEP_ONE/KING_V2_MERGE_ROUND 380, and let hostless small dragons walk toward the last heard king instead of
  splitting.
