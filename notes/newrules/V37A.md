# v37a: PLAN steps 1, 3, 4, 7 behind flags (2026-10-01, unswbc 1.2.3)
variants/v37a = copy of variants/v36 plus four new flags in params.hpp (all default 0). Code is in main.cpp.
- QKILL (step 1): tryQueenKill(), called from tryRam() right after the units<2 guard, for non-queens only. It finds the enemy queen's head (id<=1, other team) and runs an iterative-deepening DFS of up to K=F+(L-2) steps. Each step is checked against a simulated body: a free step frees 1 tail tile, a paid step frees 2, eating holds 1, and a paid step needs L>=3. Intermediate tiles must be visible and either empty or ours. The search is pruned by BFS distance to her head and capped at 20k nodes. It skips worth, ramGuard, RAM_MIN_UNITS and QH_MIN_UNITS.
- KREACH (step 3): computeThreats plus replyEnemyReach (which is inert while REPLY_SEARCH=0). Reach is min(K(Lv), KR_CAP=4), with Lv>=3 when the tail is unseen. It applies to our dragons with L>=5 (paid tier, x THREAT_PAID_MULT) and to the queen (full threat).
- QSTATE (step 4): qstUpdate() and qstWord(). A QSECRET sonar word on the idle rays carries the queen beacon, relays (3 hops), enemy-queen sightings and a 1-bit "their queen dead" flag. A queen beacon sent as a king (SECRET, id<=1) also counts. We mark their queen dead when we saw her head last turn, that tile is in view and empty now, and no part of her is visible. Modes: the queen uses queenSafe() once theirs is dead or from round QS_LATE=400. When ours is dead (silent > QUEEN_ALIVE_MEM) and theirs is alive, QKILL turns on and dragons within 12 tiles of her last tile get -20*dist added to their move score.
- KREACH_KING (step 7): kings (L>=8) see enemies of L>=17 at F(Lv), past KING_THREAT_REACH. The KING_V2 keep-away becomes max(keep, F(enemy)+1).

## Combo vs v36 (on the coordinator's instruction, after v36 went live)
v37a defaults were then set back to v36's: QUEEN_SAFE=1, FREE_FIX=1, THREAT_FREE=1, QUEEN_HUNT=1. With all four new flags at 0, v37a is v36.
Combo QKILL+QSTATE+KREACH+KREACH_KING vs v36: P .512 (43/84; portals .57, every other map .50). U pooled .500 (gen .49, lesson .58, gated .50, sprint .29 (8/28), holdout .54).
The combo does not pass. Sprint .29 is the warning sign, and 28 games is a small sample.
Compute (--sandbox, test copy variants/v37a_combo with all four flags 1, seed 3): schooltime max 14.5M per turn (mean 6.8M), dilemma max 5.8M. Well under 40M.

## Recommendation
Keep all four new flags at 0 (v37a = v36 behaviour). None of them shows a gain at this sample size. Two leads are worth more seeds before a server A/B: KREACH_KING (U .536, P flat) and QKILL (U .525). Trophy .29 under KREACH (step 3) fits VERIFY: small-dragon trades are even, so the extra caution costs on trophy.
