# v70 = v61 + per-map Sprint king seed/feed (Around UNSW 190/230), 2026-10-04

Fix 2 of notes/newrules/LOSS_AUDIT2.md. Directory variants/v70 (cp of v61). Nothing submitted, no challenges.
The non-queen king is unchanged (this is not QK_BIG). With `-DSPRINT_KING_PM=0` v70 = v61.

## What changed
In `applyMapParams` (main.cpp ~99) the Sprint branch now overrides seed/feed (and optionally the any-length merge
round) per map after setting UNK_KING_SEED 250 / UNK_KING_FEED 300:
- Around UNSW: `PM_AUNSW_SEED 190`, `PM_AUNSW_FEED 230` (on by default)
- Islands, Australia: `PM_ISL_*` / `PM_AUS_*` exist but default to 250/300 (= v61)
- New `RP.mergeRound` replaces the constant KING_V2_MERGE_ROUND (380) in shouldFeed. `PM_*_MERGE` default 380 (= v61).
Params at the end of params.hpp: SPRINT_KING_PM 1, PM_AUNSW_SEED/FEED/MERGE, PM_ISL_SEED/FEED/MERGE, PM_AUS_SEED/FEED/MERGE.

## Why the problem is fragmentation, not only timing
Top-5 lengths at r500 in v61 self-play on AUNSW often look like 45/42/34/26 or 50/34/31/29/26: several kings grow side
by side. Losing the longest race while out-massing 1.5x fits that.

## Reading
- When we out-mass the opponent (the AUNSW server loss pattern), the earlier seed/feed gives a much longer king
  (+15 vs v35). Against an equal-mass opponent (v44) it is worse: fewer foragers, and the opponent's king wins more often.
  Self-play vs v61 is about neutral on longest.
- Merge 320 (big dragons may feed from r320 instead of r380) is mildly positive in all three settings but small.
  Not on by default; possible follow-up `-DPM_AUNSW_MERGE=320`.
- Weaker candidate than v69. If tested, judge on AUNSW only: the both-queens-dead longest-lost count (audit: 10 of 17
  AUNSW losses) and the longest-dragon gap. With ~1/17 of games on AUNSW it needs many games to show anything in the
  overall rate.
