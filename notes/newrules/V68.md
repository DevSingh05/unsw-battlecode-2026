# v68 = v61 + LATE_HUNT (hunt the enemy queen once ours is dead), 2026-10-04
Directory: variants/v68 (cp -R variants/v61, plus the v59 EQ_HUNT tracker code, which has EQ_HUNT=0 here).
Also in `bot/` as **`LATE_HUNT`**, default 0. With the flag off, bot/ plays exactly like v61: the event streams were
identical on devil, sprint/australia and slithery_fight at seed 101. With `-DLATE_HUNT=1`, bot/ plays exactly like v68.
Nothing was submitted and no challenges were sent.

Target: LOSS_AUDIT2 fix 1. Against non-top-10 teams, 45% of our losses are r500 games where our queen is dead and
theirs is alive. Once our queen is dead, r500 is lost unless her queen dies too, so a hunt costs little.

## What it does (params.hpp "---- v68" block)
1. **"Our queen is dead" signal, from positive evidence only.**
   - A non-queen saw our queen's head on its previous turn. Now that tile is in view and no part of her is visible, so she died.
   - The dragon then floods a notice (LH_SECRET word with the death round) on its idle rays. It sends every turn for 20 rounds, then every 5th round. Notices may displace king relays one round in three.
   - Seeing her alive again cancels it (no false alarms seen).
   - Nothing is sent while she is alive. The game is bit-identical to v61 until a dragon sees her die.
2. **Enemy-queen tracker.** The v47 qstUpdate code runs passively all game. It records her sightings, length, facing and death.
   Once our queen is known dead, sightings and "enemy queen dead" notices are relayed on idle rays. The notice goes
   every 4th round, not just for 10 rounds.
3. **The hunt (lhPrep)** runs when our queen is known dead, theirs is not known dead, and LH_EVID holds: someone saw her
   at or after our queen's death.
   - Hunters: non-king units with L<=8, or any length if her largest seen length is <=4.
   - They pay LH_W=6 per step of map-wide BFS distance, capped at 60. If she is out of BFS range, the cost is straight distance + 4.
   - Her threat zone is ignored, because a head-on with her kills both.
   - Target: her last sighting if it is younger than 60 rounds and I haven't checked her tile. Otherwise, while her last sighting is younger than 120 rounds, a sweep of the enemy spawns on known maps. Her spawn comes first, then the other spawns, spread by id stride.
   - The king may also take a visible queen kill (tryQueenKill).
4. **Off by default: LH_BEACON** (queen alive beacon + sparse relays + key-salted king words + 40-round silence = dead).
   It caught the unseen deaths, but it gave false alarms in many games (Trauma, AUNSW, Slithery, Maze, Weakhold, from about r65).
   The queen's rays mostly hit kelp. Kept only for reference.

Not built: a finisher for when the enemy is down to a few units while our queen lives. It would break the "no change
while she lives" rule. Also, the audit's 20 small-opponent losses all had our queen dead, so the hunt already covers them.

## Why LH_EVID (round 1 to round 2)
- Round 1 had no evidence gate. Overall it scored 60/136 vs v61's 68/136 (both sides, 4 seeds).
- All of the loss was in games where *their* queen died first, unseen by most of our units, before ours died. We then hunted a dead queen, and the units feeding the king walked into enemy land. Australia went from 216 to 37 total mass.
- Requiring a sighting at or after our queen's death fixed it: "their queen died first" games went 31 W for v61 to 32 W for v68.

## Results (local, vs v61, 17 ranked maps x seeds 101-104 x both sides = 136 games)

- Self-play can't show the target effect. v61's queen flees heads and v61 already kills most queens (32/40). The audit's target is weaker teams' queens that sit in view for 100+ rounds.
