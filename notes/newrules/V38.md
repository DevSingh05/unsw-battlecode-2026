# v38: v37d + UNK_SCARCE_FIX + UNK_PORTAL_EXPLORE (+ QALLY_KEEP, off), 2026-10-01
Base: copy of variants/v37e with QGUARD default 0. The server result was v37e 19/50 vs v37d 29/50 (top-10), so v38 with the new flags off is v37d.

## Code (variants/v38)
- main.cpp: the q36 -> bot/ diff (UNK_SCARCE_FIX, UNK_PORTAL_EXPLORE, logPending; 191 lines) applied cleanly with `patch`. The .orig file went to archive/v38_build/.
  The queen is never a scout: UPE keeps `w.myId > 1`. SCARCE_FIX only enables pathing through known pairs, and that applies to every dragon.
- params.hpp, new block at the end: UNK_SCARCE_FIX 1 (USF_RICH 4), UNK_PORTAL_EXPLORE 1 (UPE_HUNGER 25, UPE_MOD 4), QGUARD 0.
- QALLY_KEEP (default **0**) + QAK_PEN 150, QAK_DIST 2, QAK_ESC_DEPTH 2, QAK_CAP 24, QAK_FROM 10.
  It applies to a non-queen ally when our queen's head is seen this turn, or a kingsSeen beacon with id<=1 is from this or the last round, and the ally is within 5 of her.
  It scores -QAK_PEN for ending the step within Manhattan 2 of her head, or on her best escape tiles. Those are her open first step with the largest small flood (cap 24; ties are all kept), plus the tiles up to 2 further on.
  It is exempt when feedPhase() && shouldFeed() && the king is the queen (id<=1).
- Line 1 of main.cpp names the variant. **Tool gotcha:** the `unswbc --sandbox` wasm cache key hashes only the .cpp files (clangtool.sources). An edit to params.hpp alone reuses the stale wasm. Touch main.cpp's content after editing a header before a sandbox run.

## Recommendation
v38 as shipped (QALLY_KEEP=0) = v37d plus the unknown-map portal fixes, pooled .509 vs v37d with no set below .5. On ranked maps it is identical to v37d.
If QALLY_KEEP is retried, gate it to known maps (portals) or to late game, or use a smaller penalty/escape set.
