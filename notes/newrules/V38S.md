# v38s = v38m + the 7 Sprint maps known

Built 2026-10-02. Directory: variants/v38s (copy of v38m; v38m untouched).

## What changed
- maps_data.hpp: 33 maps (was 26). Added Around UNSW, Australia, Islands, Maze, Stripes, Tower Defense,
  weakhold from maps/sprint/*.map (unswbc 1.2.7; around_unsw.map has MAP_NAME "Around UNSW").
  Generated by scratchpad genmaps3/genmaps_out.py (genmaps2 copy with sprint1..7 added to ranked_ids,
  so Sprint maps sort with the ranked ones). Source set = genmaps2/src + <Name>__sprintN.map.
  932 KB header (was 657 KB). Binary 713 KB (was 547 KB). Compile ~2 s.
- params.hpp flags:
  - SPRINT_KNOWN=1. 0 = Sprint maps are skipped as candidates in World::init (behaves exactly as v38m).
    1 = adopt them, and applyMapParams(name, tiles) gives them kingDeclare=1, kingSeedRound=UNK_KING_SEED,
    feedStart=UNK_KING_FEED, plus ramGuard=2 when w*h <= 750 (Stripes 288, Tower Defense 512, weakhold 600).
  - SPRINT_KING_FULL=1: on a known Sprint map also use UNK_KING's shrinking feed reserve (feedReserveNow)
    and the crown-early rule (units >= unitLimit-2).
  - SPRINT_ROUTE=0 (untested): 1 keeps PEARL_ROUTE=2 route valuation on known Sprint maps.
    v38m had route valuation there because the maps were unknown; v38s turns it off by being known.
  - SPRINT_DEBUG=0: 1 prints ADOPT/DROP lines to stderr.
- Other !w.known paths now off on Sprint maps (that is the point of knowing them): UNK_SCARCE_FIX,
  UNK_PORTAL_EXPLORE, UNK_EXPLORE, EXPLORE_LEARN, UNK_BLIND_PEN, PEARL_ROUTE (see SPRINT_ROUTE).

## Checks
- SPRINT_KNOWN=0 replays byte-identical to v38m (binaries renamed to equal-length names) on
  maps/sprint/stripes.map and maps/devil.map, seeds 1 and 2, both bot orders.
- Identification (SPRINT_DEBUG=1, seed 1): all 7 maps, both bot orders, on the official file and a copy with
  DRAGON teams swapped (scratchpad/swapmaps): every ADOPT names the right map, 0 DROPs. Australia: one dragon
  first adopts Around UNSW (same 64x64 size), corrected on the same turn by the countdown filter.
- Sandbox (unswbc --sandbox, fresh build): australia v38s max 16.4M (v38m 35.6M), maze max 7.3M,
  around_unsw max 13.5M. All far under 40M.
