# v39 = v38 + QSPLIT_ROOM + KV2_ENDMERGE (both off by default), 2026-10-02
Source: notes/newrules/V37D_LOSSES.md (queen dies HitSelf at L2-3 soon after a split; our late length is spread over 2-3 dragons).
Test copies: variants/v39a (QSPLIT_ROOM=1), v39b (KV2_ENDMERGE=440), v39ab (both). Flags live at the end of params.hpp.

## Flags
- QSPLIT_ROOM (QSR_A=16, QSR_ALLY=2): `qsrBlock(k)` in main.cpp. For amQueen() only, a split is refused if an ally head is
  within wrap distance 2 of her head, or splitSideArea(false, k, 16) < 16 (her own side after the split). Applied at the
  keepLen peel (SPLIT 2), PF early split, PF split (pfBlock), the normal split block after k is final (covers the tip
  k=L-2 branches), and tryEchoHold. Not applied to the trap reverse-split or the king escape split (emergency moves).
- KV2_ENDMERGE=R: from round R, in updateKingship() KING_V2 kingSelf branch: the queen never steps down; any other king
  steps down if bestK >= myLen+2 (and myLen/2 + bestK > myLen), or if a fresh queen-king beacon (id 0/1, <= 8 rounds old)
  has length >= myLen - KV2_QDIFF(2); such a dragon also won't re-crown. shouldFeed(): from R the queen never feeds, the
  units > feedReserveNow() check is dropped, and a queen-king of length >= myLen - 2 is a valid feed target.

## Checks
- Compile: all five dirs build cleanly.
- Flags off: v39 vs v38 replays (seed 1000, devil + slithery_fight) are byte-identical to v38 vs v38 except one header
  byte (the bot path).
- Sandbox (v39ab vs v38, seed 1000): slithery max 8.0M/turn, schooltime max 14.5M/turn (limit 40M).

## Disaster check (pool.py-equivalent with replays: slithery_fight, portals, devil, schooltime, seeds 1000-1003, both sides)
| cand | score | 95% |
| v39ab | 17/32 = .531 | [.364, .691] |

| v39b | 16/32 = .500 | [.336, .664] |
No disaster, no signal. Most slithery/schooltime/devil pairs are exact mirrors (the flags changed nothing there); portals is where games differ.

Queen stats (sum over 32 games, cand side vs v38 side in the same games):
| | queen splits | left her L<=3 | L<=3 with ally head <=2 | queen died after r12 | queen alive r500 |
| v39a | 97 | 87 | 8 | 19 | 5 |
| v38 (vs v39a) | 111 | 101 | 20 | 17 | 7 |
| v39ab | 97 | 87 | 8 | 19 | 5 |
QSPLIT_ROOM cuts ally-adjacent queen splits 20 -> 8 but the queen still splits to L2 (87 times) and does not survive more.
The 8 left over are probably allies that moved in earlier in the same round (the replay check uses the round-start position).
On slithery the queen still dies at r5 in every game (spawn pocket), so the flag can't help there.

KV2_ENDMERGE (instrumented copy, seed 1000): non-queen king step-downs after r440: slithery 4, schooltime 19, portals 8.
Replay proxy (late deaths of L>=4 next to a longer ally after r440): v39b 50 (358 length) vs v38 54 (399). So no extra merging.
Most kings at r430-500 hear no other king (bestK=0, kingId=-1). The two big dragons are too far apart to hear each other,
so a step-down rule can't merge them. A schooltime L58 king stepped down twice to bestK=60, which may be a stale beacon
from up to KC_STEP_MEM=30 rounds back. Merging them needs a path-to-king plan, not a step-down rule.

Verdict: neither flag is worth a server slot as it stands. If one is tried, v39a is safer (it only ever refuses splits).
