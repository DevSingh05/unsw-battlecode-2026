# v41 = v40 + FREE_STEPS (free multi-step moves), 2026-10-02
Source: notes/top5/CUTLERY_SSS_WHQ.md (top teams multi-step on 50-77% of L>=5 turns; v40 0.2-0.3%), notes/queen/FREEMOVES.md (idea A), PLAN.md S5.
Default: FREE_STEPS=1 in variants/v41/params.hpp. `-DFREE_STEPS=0` gives v40 behaviour (replays checked byte-identical).

## What changed (main.cpp)
- The per-move scoring loop in decide() is now a lambda `evalDir(d, EvalOut&)` (same code, same rng order). It also returns
  the mapScore part, and `rskip` when replyPenalty ran out of budget. The 1-step loop fills candScore/candThreat/... from it as before.
- `tryFreePath()` (called just before the final `emitMove(bestD)`, only for myLen>=5, not pfIn, no holdTile): beam search over
  free paths of 2..F steps, F = min(ceil(L/4), FS_MAXF=4). Never paid steps. Level 1 = the 4 normal candidates; each kept prefix
  (FS_BEAM=3 per depth) is applied to a virtual body (`fsApply`: head/trail/occId/freeT/freeOther patched, tail releases one tile
  per non-eating step, eating freezes it, eaten pearls cleared) and its next step is scored by the unchanged evalDir, then
  `fsRestore`. Path score = endpoint evalDir + mapScore of earlier steps + FS_EAT_W (8.2) per pearl eaten before the endpoint.
- Path legality = engine rules: each step checked against the body as the previous step left it (own tail occupied until it
  moves), other dragons fixed during our turn, no kelp. All path tiles must be visible (no blind portal steps).
- Endpoint vetoes relative to the chosen 1-step move: no threatAt if the 1-step has none; no new threatPaidAt (FS_PAID_VETO);
  depth >= min(1-step depth, SURVIVE_DEPTH); queen dist veto and QGUARD dead-end veto kept; FS_VIS: endpoint neighbours must
  be in view, unseen tiles 2 away cost FS_BLIND_PEN=3 each (queen: veto). Taken only if score > 1-step score + FS_MARGIN (0.5).
- Expansion stops at FS_BUDGET_M=20M points into the turn. Rams, feeding, eat sprints, splits, king escape, echo hold, trap
  split and the lethal fallback all run before it, unchanged. Map hooks' schRusher/traRusher are saved/restored.

## Results
- Compiles; FREE_STEPS=0 vs v40 replays identical (seed 1000, devil + sprint/islands; only the bot-path byte differs).
- Sandbox (seed 1000, v41 vs v40) max points/turn: schooltime 27.8M (p99 24.9M), sprint/australia 32.4M (p99 28.5M),
  trauma 15.2M. v40 side: 14.4 / 19.3 / 8.8M. All < 40M.
- Multi-step rate of L>=5 moves (5 local games): 50% overall (slithery 48, portals 62, australia 52, schooltime 53, trauma 19).
  Queen 25% (her penalties keep her still more). Paid steps 1 in 3365. No multi move ever died mid-move. Head-on deaths the turn
  after a move: 7/3365 multi vs 13/3375 single. Pearls eaten by L>=5: 906 on multi turns vs 421 on single.
- First (unguarded) build had 20 head-on deaths after 1859 multi moves on schooltime (walking to the edge of view / into paid
  reach); FS_PAID_VETO + FS_VIS cut that to about 1 per 1500.
- pool.py vs v40, 14 maps x 3 seeds x 2 sides: **53/84 = .631 [.524, .726]**. Low maps: queen_of_spades 2/6, dilemma/trophy/
  devil/australia 3/6; slithery, trauma, maze 5/6.
- unseen_eval vs v40 (seed 261000): gen .525, lesson .771, gated .575, holdout .537, **pooled .585 (145/248)**.
