# v40x = v40 + ENDMERGE_NEAR + QCROWN (both off by default), 2026-10-02
Sources: notes/top5/SPONGE_HORSE.md (Sponge's late suicide-merge, queen feeding), V39.md (KV2_ENDMERGE failed: kings too far apart
to hear each other), V37D_LOSSES.md (r500 losses with our length split over 2-3 big dragons). Flags are at the end of params.hpp.

## Flags
- ENDMERGE_NEAR=R (EMN_MIN 8, EMN_R 8, EMN_GAP 2, EMN_MEM 3, EMN_ENEMY_R 4, EMN_PULL 8, EMN_TOP 1). `emnPick()` + the feed block
  in decide(). From round R, a non-queen of L >= EMN_MIN picks the longest allied king that it sees (a visible head, or a king beacon
  <= EMN_MEM rounds old), that is >= myLen+EMN_GAP, within EMN_R (wrap Manhattan), with no enemy head within EMN_ENEMY_R of it, and
  (EMN_TOP) no shorter than the longest king heard since our birth. It steps down (kingSelf=false), pulls to the king's
  FEED_AHEAD tile (EMN_PULL per BFS step) and uses the KING_V2 spot/loose dieNow rule (die beside or 1-2 ahead of the head, king
  visible, no enemy head within 4). Works on all maps, not only kingDeclare ones.
  Ghost guard (needed): big dragons reverse-split often, so the child that now carries the body heard its own parent's L+2 beacon
  and suicided next to the parent's L2 head (first build: an L35 died that way on schooltime). Beacons from before our birth are
  ignored, and a visible head with < 5 visible segments counts its beacon only if it is <= 1 round old.
- QCROWN=R (QC_MIN 4, QC_ALIVE_MEM 12, QC_CAP 0). `qcUpdate()`. From round R our queen crowns herself if L >= QC_MIN and the enemy
  queen is not in view at >= her length. v40 has QSTATE=0, so there is no enemy-queen tracking beyond that. Allies set qcOn when
  they hear an id<=1 king beacon dated >= R, then reuse the QUEEN_KING machinery: no rival kings, feed/relay her, KC consensus
  bypassed for her, QGUARD ally keep-out waived for her feeders. A crowned queen makes no voluntary splits and only reverse-splits
  with no legal move. QC_CAP=N: she gives the crown back at L >= N (a Trauma pen crown is kept).

## Checks
- Compiles with every flag combination. Flags off: v40x vs v40 on trauma and schooltime (seed 1000) has identical moves, deaths
  and sonar values to v40 vs v40 (the replay bytes differ only in the bot path and timing).
- Sandbox, both on (EMN_R 20, QC_CAP 10) vs v40, seed 1000: schooltime max 14.4M/turn, slithery 7.9M/turn (limit 40M).

## What happened
- ENDMERGE_NEAR rarely fires. Trauma, autarky and slithery games were exact mirrors of v40. Late top-two distance in the seed-1000
  games (71 samples at r400/440/480, second dragon L>=8): <=8: 12, 9-16: 10, 17-30: 32, >30: 17. Most pairs are out of range, and
  v40's own KING_V2 feeding already merges any L >= 8 that hears a longer king after r380. Late growth r450->r500 on 6 maps: +10.2
  (R 20) vs +9.5. Portals is the only map that moved (6/8 with both radii). Pearls drop only on even segments, so a merge gives the
  king L/2 at most.
- QCROWN: fed queens grow (L17-51 by r375-490), then die. (a) Head-on by an enemy L2-4 head (the opponent v40 hunts queens with
  QKILL; a head-on kills both whatever the length). (b) A big queen coils, has no legal move, and the trap reverse-split hands her
  body to a child, leaving an L2 queen that dies (7 cases, e.g. trauma 463 L39 -> L2). QC_CAP=10 removes most of (a)/(b): queen
  alive 17 vs 20, maze 6/8, slithery queen 4.5 vs 0.6. But australia 2/8 and trauma 3/8 (allies drop rival kings when they hear
  the Trauma pen queen's beacon; v40 already feeds her there).

## Verdict
Neither flag is worth a server slot. EMN with EMN_R 20 is the only non-negative one (.562 known, .468 unseen = noise).
If revisited: merging needs a path-to-king plan over 17-30 tiles (relay the king's position), and QCROWN needs QSTATE tracking
plus a Trauma exclusion before another test.
