BOTTOM LINE
Stockfish is not better than us early, and it doesn't harvest better per head. It wins on four things:
 (1) it commits to ONE king 60-280 rounds earlier and feeds it continuously;
 (2) it cuts its unit count to turn swarm mass into king length;
 (3) its L5 dragons hunt our big dragons with 3-5 step sprint rams;
 (4) on the big boards (Slithery, Schooltime, Autarky) it keeps much more of the mass it harvests.
(1)-(3) are map-agnostic. (4) applies to any map with pockets or a unit cap.

BEHAVIOURS, RANKED BY HOW MUCH OF THEIR ADVANTAGE THEY EXPLAIN
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EARLY, COMMITTED KING + CONTINUOUS FEED CONVEYOR (general: YES; biggest single factor)
   What they do:
   - One dragon becomes the king at ~L6-12, around r250 by default and earlier on Portals and Autarky (r110-240).
   - From then until ~r480 a steady stream of small dragons commits suicide 2-4 tiles from the king's head:
     92% HitSelf (reversing into their own neck) with 2 free exits, feeder length median 3 (ours 2).
   - The king walks over the pearls 1-3 rounds later. It absorbs about one pearl every 2-3 rounds: on Portals 646840
     it went L6 at r255 -> L18 at r290 -> L30 at r329 -> L49 at r450 (kingwatch.py).
   - Feeders arrive from ~10 tiles away (distance 12 twenty rounds before death), exactly like ours. The mechanism is the
     same as ours; the difference is WHEN it starts, and that it never stops.
   Evidence:
   - Round-limit games vs us: own-corpse pearls eaten by L>=10 dragons per 50 rounds: SFu 10.0/10.7/12.6/13.2/12.9
     (r250 .. r500) vs U 4.1/7.3/6.0/7.8/9.8.
   - Portals: 5/6 losses. We had equal or more total length at r500 (75-88 vs 59-87) but a king of 25-31 vs 39-64.
   - Trauma 649072 and 651745: we had 36 units / 119 total at r450 and a king of 32; Stockfish had 6 units / 60 total and
     a king of 42 (final 37 vs 46 and 24 vs 54).
   What we do (variants/v31/main.cpp applyMapParams + KING_V2 table):
   - Portals: seed 250, feed 300, split stop 360. Real onset ~r315, and before r300 our best candidates keep splitting
     themselves: 646840 has split[544 L7 k5] at r273 and split[673 L6 k4] at r270.
   - Autarky: seed 380 (PM_AUT_KS) with FEED_START 330. Trauma: seed 450 (PM_TRA_KS). Global FEED_START is 330.
   Impact: the dominant cause of our losses on Portals and Trauma, and a contributor on Slithery and Autarky. Against
   other strong teams our r500 king share is 0.31 vs their 0.47, so this is a general gap.
   Try: KING_V2-style seed ~200-230 and feed ~240-250 on every map where the game usually goes to r500 (Portals, Trauma,
   Autarky, Schooltime; Slithery already 170/250). Don't let the seeded king split, except for U-turns.
   Test only on the server against top teams: self-play can't measure a king race (notes/16 §7.2 caveat).

2. SWARM DRAWDOWN: CONVERT UNITS INTO KING MASS FROM r300 (general: YES)
   What they do:
   - Round-limit units: 45.8 (r200) -> 39 (r300) -> 25.7 (r400) -> 17.5 (r500), while total length RISES 132 -> 157.
   - Small dragons (L<=3) alive: 14 (r350) -> 5.9 (r400) -> 3.0 (r450).
   - They also merge secondary big dragons into the king: merges 0.44/game vs our 0.23, and the king eats 0.38 of their
     pearls vs 0.24 for ours.
   - Their normal splits stop earlier: r401-500 k=2 splits 8 vs 30.
   What we do:
   - Units stay ~25-30 until r450 (U 26.2 at r400, 23.8 at r450); 8.8 small dragons at r450.
   - LATE_SPLIT_ROUND keeps small dragons splitting to r450, and FEED_RESERVE holds some back.
   - So a large part of our mass is still in the swarm at r500: U n_end 25 vs SFu 10.6, with equal total length.
   Impact: large. It is the second half of item 1: our king has less to eat.
   Try: once the king is committed, feed with all dragons of L<=5 outside a small reserve of 2-3 escorts or insurance kings.
   Check the r400-450 split rules (LATE_SPLIT_ROUND) against this; the evidence here says stop earlier, not later.

3. BIG-DRAGON HUNTING WITH SPRINT RAMS (general: YES)
   What they do:
   - Their L4-8 dragons (median L5 before the sprint) close in over 5-10 rounds: distance 11 -> 7 -> 3.
   - They then ram our L>=10 head with a 3-5 step sprint from distance 3-5.
   - Steps used against us: 3:9, 4:7, 2:4, 5:3, 1:1. Other strong teams mostly ram us with 1-3 steps from distance 1-3.
   - 25 of our 51 L>=10 deaths vs Stockfish were such rams (0.62/game); victim mean L16, up to L47 (642793 r437).
   - Each one gives them ~8 pearls and removes a king or insurance king.
   - They also ram our small and medium dragons after r380: 3.7/game, rammer median L4-5, mostly our rank-4+ dragons
     (feeders). Our late rams: 1.7/game, only L<=3 (RAM_MAX_OWN_LEN 3).
   - Endgame logic: a dragon about to be fed is worth only ~50% of its length to the king, so trading it for an enemy
     feeder or big dragon is cheap for them.
   What we do:
   - computeThreats (main.cpp ~2023) gives an enemy head reach = visible length - 1, capped at KING_THREAT_REACH 4.
   - If the enemy's tail is out of view the floor is THREAT_TAIL_UNKNOWN 2. A Stockfish L5 whose tail is off-screen looks
     like reach 2, while it actually sprints 4.
   - KING_ESCAPE reacts only when a threat is already adjacent.
   Impact: medium-large. It decides several Slithery and Schooltime games; 642793 lost six L10-47 dragons in r324-437.
   Try:
   - For L>=10: THREAT_TAIL_UNKNOWN 4 (or reach = min(4, 2 + unseen)).
   - Keep >=5 tiles from any enemy head with an unknown tail, or use enemy_head sonar echoes as an early warning.
   - Endgame: let L<=5 non-king dragons ram enemy heads (RAM_MAX_OWN_LEN 5 after the feed start).

4. MASS RETENTION AT THE UNIT CAP / PUMP QUALITY (general: partial - pocketed or capped maps; extends notes/22, notes/23)
   What they do on Slithery (both teams reach 64 units by r70-125):
   - They don't split at L4 by default. Parent L: 4:187, 5:81, 6:60, 7:23, 8+:37 per game (r61-300). Ours: 4:574, 5:52,
     6+:16.
   - Children with k>=3: 150/game vs 18.
   - Splits 388 vs our 643; deaths 389 vs 648; dragons dying within 5 rounds of birth 127 vs 257.
   - Pump splits from 0 free exits: the child dies within 5 rounds 44% of the time and eats 2.9 (ours 56% and 1.95).
   - Result: total length r150 215 vs 154 and r300 ~230 vs ~160, although we EAT more (r61-150: 641 vs 465).
     Our death length in r61-150 is 613 vs 377.
   - Their mass comes from dying less, not eating more.
   - Autarky: they eat 77 pocket (hot) pearls in r151-300 vs our 34 (notes/22 story; POCKET_FACTORY only partly
     closes it).
   Impact: explains the Slithery economy gap and part of Schooltime. Previous generic fixes (SPLIT_VIABLE, PUMP_TIP2)
   failed; the new, specific lever is "near the cap, split at L5-7 into k>=3 children".

5. EVASIVE / POSITIONAL SPRINTS (general: YES; medium-low, mostly preserves units)
   - Their L<=4 dragons make 13.5 non-ram sprints/game (half move away from or level with the nearest enemy head, 1.9 eat).
   - Their L5-9 dragons make ~7 sprints/game (away 1.9, eat 1.4, kill 1.8).
   - Ours: small dragons sprint ONLY to ram (13.4/game, all kills); L5-9 only 0.4/game.
   Try: allow 2-step escape sprints for L>=4 when every 1-step move is inside the threat set (KING_ESCAPE for non-kings).

6. KING NAVIGATION BY U-TURN SPLITS (general: YES; low-medium)
   - Big dragons (L>=8) reverse direction with SPLIT L-2 (the child takes all but 2 segments).
   - Stockfish: 4.2/game before r300, 2.9 in r300-399, 5.1 at r400+. Many with 2-3 free exits, so they use it to steer
     (turn back towards corpses or away from rammers), not just to escape.
   - Ours: 0.7 / 3.1 / 3.4, nearly all with 0-1 exits.
   - The king's id changes each time (the Portals king 1517 was born at r353 from L47 k45). Any king-identity logic
     (KING_CONSENSUS) must survive this.

7. SONAR MESH (general: YES; enabler for 1-3)
   - Every dragon casts ~4 rays per turn: 34-37k pings/game vs our 4-5k. Payloads are NOT encrypted.
   - The sender's head is x = bits 53-58, y = bits 46-51 (e.g. 0x33c28e09... at (30,10)).
   - A second message type (low word ending 0x...00001) relays other positions, and a field shared by many senders
     (e.g. 0x5aa1...) looks like king state.
   - Every ally that is hit therefore learns positions of other dragons and of the king each turn.
   - We could read their beacons for free: head positions of their dragons, including the king, through portals and out
     of vision.

8. PORTALS (general; already known, notes/16): they cross ~35% less and die crossing about half as often
   (r151-300 fatal 3.5 vs 6.7).

NOT THEIR EDGE (don't chase)
- Opening r0-60: first split 3.7 vs 3.0; they have fewer units and less length than us at r60 (SFu 15/36 vs U 20/47).
- Harvest per head: spawn eats per head-round are equal on every map. We eat more on Slithery and Portals.
- King safety from their own mistakes: king self-deaths 0.15/game on both sides. Their L78 king died trapped against kelp
  at r468 in 642793.
- Stockfish is WEAK early on small, aggressive maps. We eliminate them on Devil (4/4, by r39-216), Dilemma (2/2),
  Default (2/2), Trophy and QoS: r60 units Devil 1.5 vs 14, Dilemma 1 vs 9. Keep our current Devil, PD and Default play.

PER MAP TYPE (vs us)
- Portal king maps (Portals): items 1+2 alone decide it. Onset ~170-240 vs ~315.
- Big pocket or capped boards (Slithery, Schooltime, Autarky): items 4 (economy by r200), 1 and 3.
  Slithery 8/9 losses: two giants at the end (e.g. 80 + 66 vs our 38).
- Mid boards with kings (Trauma): items 1+2. We lead on mass and lose the king race (our seed is 450).
- Small, aggressive maps (Devil, Dilemma, Default, Trophy, QoS): we win; their weak opening.

SUGGESTED EXPERIMENTS (flags, default off; server A/B vs top-10 on Portals, Trauma, Slithery, Autarky)
 A. EARLY_KING: seed 200-230 and feed 240-250 on Portals, Trauma, Autarky, Schooltime; forbid king self-splits except
    U-turns.
 B. DRAWDOWN: after the feed start, every non-reserve dragon of L<=5 feeds; reserve = 2 insurance dragons of L>=8; no new
    k=2 splits after ~r380.
 C. KING_REACH4: THREAT_TAIL_UNKNOWN=4 for L>=10, plus an enemy_head echo alarm.
 D. LATE_RAM: RAM_MAX_OWN_LEN 5 for non-king dragons after the feed start.
 E. CAP_SPLIT: at >=56 units, split at L>=5 with k>=3 instead of L4 -> 2+2.
 A+B are the most important and belong together.
