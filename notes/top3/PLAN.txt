2. SHARED BEHAVIOURS WE LACK, ranked by (games won x confidence) / effort
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The top 3 disagree on WHEN to seed a king: Stockfish seeds early (r110-250); cheji and
Cutlery run a pure swarm until about r300-330. All three agree on what happens after
about r330. Splitting stops, units collapse by about 65% within about 50 rounds, and the
mass goes into one or a few big dragons. At r450-500 we still hold 22-25 units, with about
9 small dragons alive at r450. Our king share at r500 is .31 against strong teams; theirs
is .45-.47. This one gap explains most of our losses on the round-limit big boards
(Slithery, Schooltime, Portals, Autarky; head-to-head king at r475-500 is 33-35 vs 43-47).
Items A-C are three parts of one fix. Test them in that order and stack them.

A. STOP LATE SMALL SPLITS   [all 3 teams | general (king maps) | effort: trivial | conf: high]
   Data: 4-into-2+2 splits after r350: cheji 0.55/game vs ours 63. k=2 splits in r401-500:
   Stockfish 8 vs ours 30. Cutlery stops splitting at r362-400. The notes/16 premise
   behind LATE_SPLIT_ROUND=450 ("top teams keep splitting") is wrong for all current top-3
   teams. The Portals study (por32, PM_POR_LATESPLIT=0) went 5/10 vs 3/10 on the server.
   Spec: generalize bot/'s RP.lateSplitRound (bot/main.cpp:129) with a new flag
   LATE_SPLIT_KING_MAPS (default -1). When it is >= 0 and RP.kingDeclare is set,
   RP.lateSplitRound = LATE_SPLIT_KING_MAPS. Test value: 340.
     if (LATE_SPLIT_KING_MAPS >= 0 && RP.kingDeclare) RP.lateSplitRound = LATE_SPLIT_KING_MAPS;
   Leave non-king maps (Trophy, Devil, Default, Dilemma) unchanged.
   Estimate: +0.5-1 game per 10 on Slithery, Schooltime and Portals, on its own.

B. CONSOLIDATION WAVE   [cheji + Cutlery + Stockfish drawdown | general (king maps) | effort: medium | conf: med-high]
   Data: from r320-330, per-round death chance for an L<=5 dragon that can see a longer
   ally is 0.16-0.26 (cheji; 0.027 with no ally in view). Cutlery has 7.3 L4-8 feeder
   deaths per game in r300-349 vs our 1.2-2.5; its top 5 take 34% of own-corpse pearls vs
   our 16%. Stockfish L<=3 dragons fall from 14 (r350) to 3 (r450). All three reach r400
   with 14-21 units; we have 26-30.
   Spec: new flag WAVE_R (default 0, test 330), plus WAVE_MAXLEN (default 8), WAVE_RADIUS
   (default 3) and WAVE_KEEP (default 4 reserve dragons).
     bool waveFeed() const {           // checked in shouldFeed() before the KING_V2 branch
       if (!WAVE_R || !RP.kingDeclare || round < WAVE_R || iAmKing() || myLen > WAVE_MAXLEN) return false;
       if (units <= WAVE_KEEP) return false;
       // visible own head that is longer, Chebyshev <= WAVE_RADIUS (7x7 view)
       for (auto& s : segs) if (s.head && s.team==w.myTeam && s.id!=myId &&
            ownLen(s.id) >= myLen+2 && cheb(s.tile,myHead) <= WAVE_RADIUS) { waveTarget = s.id; return true; }
       return false;
     }
   Reuse the existing feed-spot mechanics, but aim at waveTarget (step onto a tile in front
   of that head, then die). Cutlery-style variant WAVE_AHEAD=1: die 3-5 tiles ahead on the
   king's path, not 1-2 tiles from it.
   While WAVE_R is on, relax the shouldFeed length cap: myLen <= 8 from WAVE_R instead of
   3/6 (v31 main.cpp:1978).
   Estimate: +1-2 games per 10 on the four big boards. The largest lever, but not tested.
   Risk: suicides next to enemy heads. Keep the existing threat checks, and never wave-feed
   when an enemy head is within 3 of the target.

C. EARLIER BIG MERGE INTO ONE KING   [cheji + Stockfish (+Cutlery) | general (king maps) | effort: trivial-low | conf: medium]
   Data: late big merges (L8+, r300+): cheji 6.2 per game with 98 length and 12.6 pearls
   to the king, vs ours 2.3 / 29 / 0.95. Stockfish merges L10+ dragons 0.44/game (ours 0.23),
   and its L10+ dragons split 6.0/game after r400 (ours 1.6). Cheji's own weakness is
   holding a top 3 too long; single-king peers beat it.
   Spec: KING_V2_MERGE_ROUND 420 -> 380 (param only; no code). Then, separately, a
   step-down: a self-declared king steps down after hearing ONE king >= myLen+max(3,myLen/4)
   within KC_STEP_MEM (KC currently needs two). New flag KC_STEP_ONE (default 0).
   Estimate: +0.5 game per 10 on the big boards. Stacks with B.

D. CAP SPLIT: BANK LENGTH IN L5-9 NEAR THE UNIT CAP   [Cutlery + Stockfish | general (cap/pocket maps) | effort: medium | conf: medium]
   Data: Slithery r61-300: children with k>=3 per game: Stockfish 150, Cutlery about 40%
   of splits, ours 18. Stillborn children 127 vs 257. Total length at r150: 215 vs 154,
   although we eat more. Cutlery makes 114 tip splits per game with child = min(L-2, 5).
   Spec: new flag CAP_SPLIT (default 0; test 56). At units >= CAP_SPLIT, the split gate
   (main.cpp:3418) needs myLen >= 5, and the child is k = min(myLen-2, CAP_SPLIT_KMAX=5)
   when the head has <= 1 free exit (tip). Otherwise k = 3.
     if (CAP_SPLIT && units >= CAP_SPLIT) { if (myLen < 5) skipSplit = true;
         else k = (freeExits(myHead) <= 1) ? std::min(myLen-2, CAP_SPLIT_KMAX) : 3; }
   Also test PF_CHILD_MAX 2 -> 5 on Autarky (a param only).
   Caution: SPLIT_VIABLE and PUMP_TIP2 were rejected. They changed WHERE we split; this
   changes WHEN and HOW BIG. Server only, on Slithery + Autarky.
   Estimate: +0.5-1 game per 10 on Slithery and Autarky.

E. BIG-DRAGON THREAT RADIUS   [Stockfish (Cutlery 3-step rams 4/game) | general | effort: low | conf: medium]
   Data: 25 of our 51 L10+ deaths against Stockfish were 3-5 step sprint rams, and our
   median victim was L16. THREAT_TAIL_UNKNOWN=2 undercounts an L5 whose tail is out of view.
   Spec: new flag THREAT_TAIL_UNKNOWN_BIG (default 0; test 4). In computeThreats
   (main.cpp:2025), use it as the floor for enemy heads with an unseen tail when
   myLen >= 10 (still capped by KING_THREAT_REACH). This is a params-level change.
   Estimate: +0.3 game per 10 against Stockfish-style teams. Watch for over-caution: king
   eats at r300-400 must not drop.

F. NARROW CORPSE SPRINT   [cheji (big), Cutlery (8.3/game eat sprints), Stockfish (small) | general (contact maps) | effort: medium | conf: low-med]
   Data: cheji eats 106 corpse pearls per game after fights vs our 71 (31 vs 0.9 by sprint).
   Cutlery reports the corpse race as even. The two teams disagree, so confidence is lower.
   Spec: new flag EAT_SPRINT_CORPSE (default 0). tryEatSprint (main.cpp:3263) fires only
   when the sprint path contains a pearl dropped <= 3 rounds ago by a death AND an enemy
   head is within 3 of it; myLen >= 3; at most 2 steps; per-step threat check. Leave
   generic EAT_SPRINT off.
   Server A/B on Schooltime + Slithery. Target: our share of the corpse race > 50%.

G. ESCAPE SPRINT   [Stockfish + Cutlery | general | effort: low-med | conf: low]

   New flag ESCAPE_SPRINT (default 0). Low expected value; do it last.
