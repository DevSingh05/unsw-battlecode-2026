BEHAVIOURS, RANKED BY HOW MUCH OF THEIR ADVANTAGE THEY EXPLAIN
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE r325 CONSOLIDATION WAVE (general, map-agnostic)
   What they do:
   - Until about r320 every dragon stays small (L2-5, top1 about 7). The team sits at the unit cap on the big boards
     (64 on Slithery and Schooltime) and does about 21 growth splits per 25 rounds.
   - At r325, on every map, splitting drops to 10 per 25 rounds, and to 3-5 after r350.
   - Small dragons then die in place (no action), next to a LONGER ally that is in their 7x7 view.
     - Death hazard per round for L<=5 in r325-360: 0.26 when an L>=10 ally is visible, 0.16 when an L5-9 ally is
       visible, 0.027 when none is. In r250-320 it is 0.015-0.03 whatever is visible.
     - The nearest visible bigger ally eats the corpse: 55% of the pearls go to that exact dragon, 92% to the team.
   - Units fall 47 -> 15 in 25 rounds (the burst peaks at r330-342) and are down to about 5 by r450, while total
     mass drops only 147 -> 120.
   - The result is a local, tournament-style merge into a TIER of medium dragons, with top3 about 18/14/12 at r350
     and 28/22/18 at r400. Nothing has to travel to one global king.
   Evidence:
   - wave.py: every round-limit game, on Slithery, Portals, Schooltime, Trauma and Autarky, starts at r320-330.
   - splitline.py: cheji 21 -> 10 -> 7 splits per 25 rounds; deaths 21 -> 42 -> 12.
   - hazard.py, localfeed.py, feedeff.py: eaters in the wave are L5-19 in 60% of cases.
   - The same pattern holds in all 20 cheji-v-us round-limit games.
   What we do instead:
   - We keep 4 -> 2+2 splitting to r450: LATE_SPLIT_ROUND 450, LATE_SPLIT_MAXLEN 5
     (variants/v31/params.hpp:550, main.cpp:3420). That makes 30-35 splits and 30-37 deaths per 25 rounds right up
     to r450, and 22-25 units at r400-450.
   - Our feeding goes to one consensus king only (shouldFeed, main.cpp:1965-1980: kingLen >= myLen+2, the
     KC_FEED_FRAC 0.75 consensus, KING_V2_FEED_LEN 3 until r380 and 6 until KING_V2_MERGE_ROUND 420), so most small
     dragons have no one to feed.
   - Their pearls recycle among L2-4 pump dragons: 63% of our own-corpse pearls in r330-500 go to dragons outside
     the top 10.
   - Conversion on Portals, a pure race with no contact: cheji turns 62% of its r325 mass into the final king
     (42/68); we turn 41% (31/76). At cheji's rate our r325 mass would make a king of about 47, which beats their 42.
   Impact:
   - This is the main reason for our round-limit losses: 16/20 against cheji, including 5/6 on Portals, where we had
     more mass.
   - Map-avg king at r475: 44.9 v 33.0.
   Suggested flag, WAVE_R=325 (behind a flag; for the bot agent to build):
   - From WAVE_R, no growth splits for L<=5. Only U-turn/escape splits stay.
   - A non-king dragon with L<=5 that sees a longer own head within Chebyshev 3 moves to the tile in front of that
     head (FEED_AHEAD logic) and dies there, with no requirement that the target is the global king.
   - Keep FEED_RESERVE about 4 "far" dragons as elimination insurance.
   - A cheap first test: LATE_SPLIT_ROUND=330 alone.
   - Test on the server: Portals (a clean king race) plus Slithery against top-10. Self-play cannot see this
     (journal: LATE_SPLIT was accepted on self-play).

2. DON'T BUILD THE KING BEFORE THE WAVE; KEEP THE SWARM HARVESTING (general)
   What they do:
   - Top1 at r300 is 7.6 (vs peers) and 7.4 (vs us); on Portals it is 4.5 at r300 and 5.8 at r325.
   - Everything stays a small harvester until the wave, and team mass keeps growing r250 -> r325 (+13 vs us,
     +10 vs peers).
   What we do instead:
   - We seed kings from r170-250 (KING_SEED_ROUND / RP.kingSeedRound, per map) and feed and pump from r250.
     Top1 at r300: 12.7 vs cheji, 18.8 on Slithery.
   - Team mass FALLS 114 -> 94 (r250 -> r325, vs cheji) because of pump churn. In r200-325: 162 trapped suicides per
     game against their 55, and own-corpse re-eats of 298 against 176, i.e. mass recycled at a loss.
   - It fits notes/23 (mass retention) and notes/22 (pump nets -0.2 per cycle).
   Impact:
   - About 35 of mass by r325 in the direct games (tot325 94 v 147).
   - On Slithery we lead the king race to r375 (25.5 v 26.5 at r375, bigger at r300) and still lose it at r450-500
     (37.5 v 46.8), because their mass pool is 40 larger.
   Caveat:
   - Trauma is the counter-example: our single early king wins 6/6 against peers there, and cheji is 1/3 on Trauma.
   - Keep the per-map king seed where it wins (Trauma), and test a later seed or no seed on Slithery and Portals only
     together with WAVE_R.

3. BIG-DRAGON MERGE INTO THE KING, r350-480 (general)
   What they do:
   - After the wave, the medium tier grows. Then the smaller big dragons (L8-32) die, with no action, within 1-2
     tiles of the king.
   - Examples: 650042 r436, an L28 dies next to the L38 king, which eats 12 of the 14 pearls. 650303 r433, an L26
     dies next to the L27 king, which eats 12.
   - Per game vs us: 6.2 such deaths (98 length); the king eats 12.6 of the pearls. Us: 2.3 deaths (29 length); the
     king eats 0.95.
   - They also U-turn big dragons: split k = L-2 and let the L2 front die at once (23.7 such L >= 10 splits per game
     after r300, 5 of them by the king). We do 13.2 big splits per game.
   What we do instead:
   - Big non-kings only feed after KING_V2_MERGE_ROUND 420, and only a king that is 2 or more longer. Self-declared
     kings step down only after hearing TWO longer kings (the notes/forensics A1 KING_MERGE_STEPDOWN proposal is not
     in v31).
   - Our big deaths after r300 are mostly HitSelf or rams, not merges.
   Impact:
   - About +12 king length per game in the direct games.
   - Their own weak point: they keep a top 3 until too late (r400 top3 38/25/23), and single-king peers beat them.
     So merge the tier into ONE king earlier: KING_V2_MERGE_ROUND of about 380 plus KING_MERGE_STEPDOWN.

4. SPRINT-GRAB OF CONTESTED AND CORPSE PEARLS (general)
   What they do:
   - About 62 sprint-eats per game (110 vs us), 11% of all their eats.
   - 44% are corpse pearls, 50% are contested (an enemy head within 3 of the pearl), and most happen in r61-350
     around fights.
   - Mostly L3-4 dragons doing 2-step moves (2581) and 3-step moves (612). Net length per sprint: -1 (1447),
     0 (871), +1 (433).
   - They pay about 1 length for tempo.
   - Result: they win the post-fight corpse race 106 v 71 per game vs us (31 of theirs by sprint) and 73 v 41 vs
     peers.
   What we do instead:
   - We sprint only to ram (EAT_SPRINT=0, params.hpp:448). It was rejected at 0.46 in v15 self-play, which cannot
     show a corpse race against a sprinting opponent.
   Impact:
   - About +35 corpse pearls per game in the direct games, and 16 of the 35-mass r200-325 gap.
   - It matters most on contact maps: Schooltime, Slithery, Autarky, Devil.
   Suggestion:
   - A narrow EAT_SPRINT: only when at least one pearl on the path is a fresh corpse pearl (dropped in the last 3
     rounds) AND an enemy head is within 3 of it, with L >= 3, max 2-3 steps, and the usual step-by-step threat check.
   - Server A/B on Schooltime and Slithery.
