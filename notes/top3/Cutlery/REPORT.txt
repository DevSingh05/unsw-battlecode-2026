BEHAVIOURS, RANKED BY HOW MUCH OF THEIR ADVANTAGE THEY EXPLAIN
------------------------------------------------------------

1. [GENERAL] A late, violent king rally: no feeding before ~r300, then collapse the swarm into one king by ~r400.
   What they do:
   - Keep the full swarm and do no king-feeding until r300-330.
     - King at r300 is only 11.0 (ours 13.2).
     - Near-king deaths r200-300: 1.2/window (ours 1.2-2.5, rising from r250).
   - Then the unit count falls about 65% within 50 rounds:
     - onset.py: steepest 50-round unit drop starts at r294-346 on every king map.
     - Units 26→8 Portals, 49→21 Schooltime, 55→19 Slithery, 40→13 Autarky, 33→12 Trauma.
   - Split stop (splits < 35% of the mid-game rate): r362-400. Ours (v28-v31): r450-489, and 381-397 on Schooltime.
   - King +15-21 within 75 rounds of the drop (ours +7-14). King eats r300-399: 21.1 vs 11.9 (kingsrc.py). They come from:
     - own corpses from feeders that died 3-6 tiles away: 17.7 vs 6.8. The feeders are mostly within 4-5 of the king and approaching it (+5.3 tiles over their last 6 rounds).
     - spawns: 2.4 vs 4.4.
   - Medium dragons are fed too: L>=4 own deaths r300-349 are 7.3/game vs ours 1.2-2.5. 23% of the corpse sources the king eats are L4-8, against 11% for us.
   - Pearls go to the top: in r300-399, 34% of own-corpse pearls are eaten by the top-5 dragons (king 9.8+9.9 per game); ours 16% (king 3.5+3.7). Our pearls go to L2-3 dragons, which churn them again.
   Result:
   - Longest on king maps: r400 31.2 vs 22.8, r500 43.0 vs 34.5.
   - Same numbers split by map (Cutlery / us):

        map            r400 king   final king
        Autarky        28          45 vs 19
        Portals        29          39 vs 31
        Schooltime     25          46 vs 29
        Slithery       44          54 vs 41

   Our current config does nearly the opposite on the big boards:
   - main.cpp KING_V2 table: Slithery seed 170 / feed 250 / split stop 370; Schooltime 200/260/340; Portals 250/300/360. LATE_SPLIT_ROUND is 450.
   - So we start feeding early, while the swarm is still growing, and keep small dragons splitting until 450-475.
   - v31 A/B games still show it:
     - Slithery+Schooltime: 51.7 voluntary (free-neighbour) own deaths per game in r100-320, against Cutlery 3.9 (churn.py).
     - The unit drop comes at r263 on Schooltime and r302 on Slithery, but the king only reaches 15-30 by r+75.
   Note: notes/16 (older top-10 sample) said "top teams keep splitting to r450 and feed from r350". Current Cutlery splits at 30/window until r325, 18 at r350, 12 at r375, 8 at r400. Treat the notes/16 LATE_SPLIT conclusion as stale for Cutlery.
   Suggested lever:
   - Per map, or better one global schedule: feedStart ~320-330, splitStop ~360-380, LATE_SPLIT_ROUND ~370.
   - Allow L4-8 feeders from r320.
   - Feed 3-5 tiles ahead on the king's path rather than 1-2 (FEED_DIST 4 is close; the KING_V2 "spot" rule forces 1-2 ahead).
   - Needs a server A/B on Slithery+Schooltime vs top-10. Self-play cannot show it.

2. [GENERAL for maps with dead ends/corridors; biggest on Slithery and Schooltime] Tip splits with variable k: bank length in L4-9 dragons instead of L2s.
   Split parent L / child k in r100-300 (share of all splits; the remaining shares are small):

      map         Cutlery                                           us / v31
      Slithery    L4→2 39%, L5→3 19%, L6→4 19%, L7-9→5 18%          L4→2 69-71%, L5→2 6%
                  (>=56 units)                                      (all at >=56 units), L5→3 2%
      Schooltime  L4→2 62%, L9→5 10%, L5→3 9%, L6→4 7%, L7→5 4%     L4→2 84%, L5+→2 12%
      Portals     L4→2 68%, L5→3 14%, L6→4 3%                       L4→2 90-95%
      Trauma      L4→2 89%, k>=3 ~6%                                L4→2 >99%

   - The rule is: keep 2 at the head, child = min(L-2, 5). The L2 parent is left at the dead-end tip and dies trapped (63% within 2 rounds); the child leaves from the old tail with L3-5.
   - Tip splits r60-320: 114/game vs us 3, v31 22.
   Result:
   - Mean unit length (lendist.py):
     - Slithery 3.1/3.6/3.8 at r150/250/320, against ours 2.5/2.6-2.7/3.9-4.0.
     - Schooltime 3.4/3.6/4.1, against ours 2.7/2.8-3.2.
     - 17-24% of their Slithery units are L4-5 and 6-10% are L6-9; ours 5-11% and 0-2%.
   - Fewer splits and less churn: k=2 splits r60-320 202/game vs ours 345-407; Slithery+Schooltime deaths r100-320 312 vs 360-382.
   - More total mass:
     - Slithery total at r250-325: 225-230 vs 144-165.
     - Schooltime: 191-208 vs 118-129.
     - At the 64-unit cap they reach 225 total with 60 units; we hit the cap with L2s.
   - Early totals r150 (early.py): Slithery 194 vs 148, Schooltime 120 vs 77, Autarky 76 vs 56.
   Our levers:
   - PUMP_TIP_SPLIT and POCKET_FACTORY with PF_CHILD_MAX (v30 ships PF_CHILD_MAX=2, i.e. 2-long children).
   - Cutlery's evidence says the child should take the rest up to 5 (PF_CHILD_MAX=0 or 5). Allowing it at high unit counts (>=56) on corridor maps is the general form: grow to L5-9, then tip-split.

3. [GENERAL] A full sprint repertoire. We only sprint to ram with L3 two-steppers.
   Sprints per game (sprints.py):
   - Cutlery: kill 13.8, eat 8.3, escape (enemy head <=4 and farther after) 5.5, other 10.4.
   - Us: kill 9.6, everything else 0.6.
   Detail:
   - 26% of their sprints are 3 steps. Ours: 0.7%.
   - 3-step rams: 4.0/game. Ours: 0 (tryRam only looks at distance 1-2).
   - L4-7 sprint rams: 2.6/game vs 0.2. In games vs us: 3.2 vs 0.07.
   - Kings sprint: 3.6 L10+ sprints per game.
   - Vs us, 18.0 of their 22.9 rams hit a dragon that was already inside their 2-3 step reach before they moved; 4.1 walked into it that turn (rams.py).
   - Our threat model is MAX_THREAT_REACH 1 for small dragons, so our L2-3s loiter inside sprint reach.
   Impact: medium. It pays mainly through corpse position and small-map fights; on QoS vs us they rammed 13.5 vs 2.5 per game.
   - EAT_SPRINT (0.46) and SMALL_SPRINT_THREAT were rejected in self-play only. Both deserve a server A/B, since self-play cannot reward them.
   - Suggested: allow a 3-step ram (and L4-7 rams when locally ahead); make reach 2 for small dragons when not locally ahead.

4. [GENERAL, strongest on large open boards] Wider early spread and harvest coverage.
   - Nearest-own-head distance at r60: 4.8 vs 3.8.
   - Share of map tiles visited by heads r0-60: 0.278 vs 0.238.
   - Distinct spawn tiles eaten r0-150: Schooltime 100 vs 60, Slithery 91 vs 81, Autarky 38 vs 33 (early.py).
   - Schooltime (zones.py): they farm the wrap strip y30-39 across the full width (82 spawn eats r0-150 vs our 19) and their home corner (50 vs 21). Our heads bunch in two mid columns.
   - The strip part is map-specific. The general part is the dispersion: a stronger crowd penalty or a per-head territory.

5. [MAP-SPECIFIC] QoS spade rooms plus ram dominance.
   - In both QoS games vs us, Cutlery ate 91-101 spawns in r61-200 (ours 2-29) and grew from 9.5 to 21 units, while we stayed at 6.
   - They rammed 13.5 vs 2.5 per game (local 3.0/1.6) and eliminated us at r179 and r225.
   - Against the field on QoS: rams 14.6 vs 3.8 received, win 4/5.

6. [GENERAL, low] Endgame hygiene.
   - Few units left, so only 0.6 own heads near the king (ours 1.7).
   - King deaths are about the same, enemy pressure on the king is the same, and they keep "lieutenants" of L15/10/8 at r400.
   - Mostly a by-product of #1.

7. [NO EDGE / THEY ARE WORSE]
   - Opening: same r0 split count (1.8), first later split r10-15. 81% k=2 in r0-60 vs our 100% (their k=3 are early tip splits). r50 units 14 vs 16. No edge.
   - Portal use: no edge.
   - Raw harvest per head-round: no edge.
   - Devil: 0/5; rams 31/26 per game with local odds 5.2/5.4, so they get out-traded.
   - Default: 2/5.

WHAT IS GENERAL (MAP-AGNOSTIC)
------------------------------
- #1 rally timing and intensity: every round-limit map, same ~r300-330 onset.
- #3 sprint repertoire and threat reach.
- #4 dispersion.
- #2 is general for any map with dead ends or corridors (tip-split rule), largest on crowded boards near the unit cap.
- #5 and the Schooltime wrap strip are map-specific.

CAVEATS
-------
- Cutlery samples are 4-8 games per map. In 23 of 31 round-limit Cutlery games there is a 25-round window with >= 4 near-king feed deaths (feed.py). The per-map mean unit-drop onset is r294-346 on every king map (onset.py).
- The "us" set is mostly v28/v29. v31 (u31) only covers 4 maps, but shows the same early feed and late split stop.
- sim2 counts L14 starters as 1 long until their first split (Autarky), and the "king" is the longest dragon, not our declared king.
- "Voluntary" death means >= 1 free neighbour at death. Our feed suicides are HitSelf into the neck.
