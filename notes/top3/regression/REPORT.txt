v31 REGRESSION CHECK (Trophy / Devil / all maps)            2026-09-30 ~02:45 UTC
==========================================================================================
Question: since v31 went live (01:06Z) Trophy 1/6 and Devil 2/7 ranked. Is KING_CONSENSUS (KC) or
ESCAPE_DEADEND_FIX (EDF) responsible, or is it the opponent mix / noise?

VERDICT
-------
Neither v31 flag caused the Trophy/Devil dip. I checked this directly: on Trophy and Devil the v31 binary
makes exactly the same decisions as v30, and as v29. The two flags cannot act there, and I also checked
every turn of every game. The dip is noise, possibly helped by a side-A effect and harder opponents. It is
not a code regression, so rolling back to v30 would change nothing on these two maps.

  1. Code path. Every KC change is gated by `RP.kingDeclare` (variants/v31/main.cpp:1758, 1841-1851,
     1973). kingDeclare is 0 on Trophy and Devil: KING_DECLARE=0, PM_TROPHY_KD/PM_DEVIL_KD=-1, and neither
     map is in the KING_V2 v2() list (Schooltime, Slithery, Portals, QoS only). EDF only runs inside
     tryKingEscape for myLen>=8. In the v31 Trophy/Devil losses no dragon of ours reached length 6.
  2. Decision replay (mechanism check). I rebuilt every one of our dragons' per-turn inputs from the replays
     (notes/autarky_calc/jam/lifeview.py) and ran them through v29, v30, v31, v31 with KC=0 and v31 with
     EDF=0, all compiled from variants/:
       Trophy, 7 v31-family games:  0 dragon lives where v31 != v30   (real == bot on 98.7% of turns)
       Devil,  8 v31-family games:  0 dragon lives where v31 != v30   (98.7%)
       Trophy+Devil, all 38 v30/v31 games: v30 vs v29 gives 0 divergent lives, except 2 L3 moves in a win.
     So v29, v30 and v31 all play Trophy and Devil the same. POCKET_FACTORY (v30) doesn't fire there either.
  3. Losses look like the v29 ones. Every v31 Trophy/Devil loss is the usual early swarm war: units level
     until r40-60, the opponent pulls ahead by r80-100, and we are eliminated at r98-233 with no dragon
     longer than 4-6. There is no king, no feeding and no step-down involved. Example:
     651473 (v31, calc) goes 17-17 at r60, 18-22 at r100, eliminated r176. That matches 629431 (v29, calc):
     16-16 at r60, 18-27 at r100, eliminated r151. Per-game profiles are in early_trophy.txt and
     gamesum_trophy.txt / gamesum_devil.txt.
  4. Opponent mix and noise.
     - Mean ranked opponent Elo: v31 2017 vs v29 1990. On Trophy the v31 opponents averaged 2024 vs 1964.
     - Against opponents rated 1990 or more, v31 is 25/45 (.56) vs v29 132/294 (.45). Devil vs 1990+:
       v29 15/30, v31 2/3. Much of v29's good Devil record came from weaker unranked opponents.
     - Opponent-matched expectation (each opponent's pre-v31 record on that map, smoothed): Trophy expected
       3.9 wins and we got 1 (P=.02). Devil expected 4.9 and we got 3 (P=.11). Both maps together:
       P=.005. That is before correcting for picking the two worst maps out of ten after looking
       (Bonferroni over 45 map pairs gives about .2). The bot did not change, so this is either luck or
       opponents playing differently. The opponents' Elo histories show no obvious jumps
       (opp_elo_history.json).
     - Side effect on Trophy: across all games since 15:30, side A is 13/26 and side B is 25/34 (vs 1990+:
       A 10/19, B 8/8). All 4 v31 ranked Trophy losses were as side A. This is worth watching, but the
       evidence is weak (about p .1).
  5. Version tagging fix. The battle 'at' time is when the battle was requested, not when it was played.
     The decision fingerprint (which binary the real moves match) shows battles 649046-50 and 649071-75
     (01:08-01:10) were still played by v30. versions.py now starts v31 at 01:15 and counts kc31 (00:54-01:05,
     v31 flags) as v31. This does not change the Trophy/Devil conclusion.

WHERE THE v31 FLAGS DO ACT (all 98 v31-family games; mech_v31.txt, mech_summary_v31.json, attrib.txt)
  Mean divergent lives per game (v31 vs v30): Slithery 88, Schooltime 58, Portals 32, Autarky 18,
  Default 10, QoS 8, Trauma 5. Devil, Trophy and Dilemma: 0.
  - Nearly every divergence comes from KC: small (L2-4) dragons from about r300 on (r190+ on Slithery),
    i.e. feeder and king-choice behaviour. In the 12 games I checked with the KC=0 binary, every divergent
    life was a KC life.
  - EDF barely fires: 19 lives in 98 games (edf_all.txt), all big dragons (L8-41) at r244-497, turning a
    1-step move into a 2-3 step sprint or a reverse split. That is harmless and rare.
  - KC effects by map, vs opponents rated 1960+:
      Slithery  v30 7/28 -> v31 10/16. Final longest 44.9 v 52.5 -> 55.5 v 50.8. A real gain.
      Portals   v30 6/19 -> v31 6/19 (feedstat), same win rate. But late feeding is down about 20%: pearls
                our L>=10 dragons ate from our own corpses after r300 went 33.6 -> 26.9 (them 38.7 -> 41.8),
                and final longest went 30.0 v 35.8 -> 28.4 v 37.9. KC's "no feed to a king under 0.75x the
                longest heard in the last 40 rounds" and "30-round step-down/no-crown memory" may be keeping
                feeders idle on Portals after a king dies or splits. In 651472 and 647117 our longest stayed
                at 3-7 from r250 to r420 (kingtrace.py). Worth a flag test (KC_FEED_MEM / KC_STEP_MEM
                shorter on Portals), but it is not the Trophy/Devil issue.
      Trauma    v30 7/9 -> v31 4/6. Losses to Stockfish (x2 incl. one v30) and Sabotage-d were king races
                lost by r300-450 (651745: our king 20 at r300 v 17, then 29 v 43 at r440). The KC
                divergences there start r369-492 and are few (5 per game), and feeding volume is unchanged
                (14.4 -> 16.2). Opponent strength, not KC.

RECOMMENDATIONS
  - Keep v31. Its gains (Slithery, and Autarky via v30) are real, and Trophy/Devil can't be affected by it.
  - Trophy/Devil weakness is the early (r40-100) swarm war vs calc / forgot to mention / Sabotage-d / Sponge.
    That needs its own study (the per-game profiles in early_trophy.txt are a starting point). Also check
    whether side A on Trophy is really weaker (mirror/frame handling, turn order).
  - Optional: A/B test KC_FEED_MEM=20 / KC_STEP_MEM=15 on Portals only, since feeding volume there dropped.
  - Tooling note: tools/ledger.py makes its battle-detail requests back to back with no rate limit.
    fetch_ledger.py here does the same job at 1 request per second and caches each battle.
