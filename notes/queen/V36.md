# v36 queen work (unswbc 1.2.3), 2026-10-01
variants/v36 = v35 + flags in params.hpp (end of file). Defaults are now QUEEN_SAFE=1, QUEEN_ALLY_THREAT=0, FREE_FIX=1 (FREE_SURVIVE follows it), THREAT_FREE=1, QUEEN_HUNT=1, QUEEN_ESCAPE=0. With every flag at 0, v36 is v35 (it went 21/42 against v35, an exact 3/6 mirror on every map).
Test set: default, schooltime, devil, trauma, gen/g2026_01, gen/g2026_02, genhold/g2027_00. Seeds 1-3, both sides, 42 games vs v35. maps/sprint dropped (no pearl spawns).
Runner: scratchpad/v36/qrun.py (replays plus queen deaths). Queen alive = our queen is alive when the game ends.

## Settled questions (6256 multi-step moves in 100 local 1.2.3 replays)
- F = ceil(L/4) is fixed at the length the turn starts with. (L4, 2 steps, 2 pearls) ends at 5, and (L4, 3 steps, 1 pearl) ends at 3. 6255/6256 moves fit len + eaten - max(0, steps - F).
- A free step onto a pearl gives +1 (L5, 2 steps, 1 pearl -> 6). A paid step onto a pearl nets 0.
- Queen ids: amQueen() = myId <= 1. "id == team index" is WRONG. On maps/sprint/islands, around_unsw and stripes, the 1st DRAGON line is team 1, so bot 0 is team B. On top of that, the server swaps starts (FRAME_FIX). The enemy queen is the seg with id <= 1 and team != ours.

## Why the win rate drops
S1+S2+S3, 84 games: eliminations went W11 / L29 (baseline 10/10). Games that reach r500 went W25 / L19 (18 of the 25 wins came from "our queen alive").
A queen that never rams or fights is one fighter fewer in the early brawls, which decide the gen maps by r16-150. The army then loses more eliminations.
Trauma: both queens often survive. v35's pen-king queen grows to 55-69, ours reaches 7-47, and we lose "longer queen" (at r491 one of ours went 33 -> 2 through a trap reverse split).
genhold g2027_00: our queen alive 0/12. She dies in L2-3 swarms, where every tile is inside some enemy's reach.

## Changes beyond PLAN / bugs found
- Bug: tools/arena.py and pool.py WIN regex expects "(by|on ...)". 1.2.3 prints "(longest dragon, 52 to 38)" and "(longer queen, ...)", so every tiebreak win scores "?" and is silently dropped. Not fixed (tools/ untouched). qrun.py uses `\(([^)]*)\)`.
- Queen threat is graded, not a flat F+1. Full penalty and hard veto inside F(L). QUEEN_PAID_PEN=120 for the tile one paid step beyond, and only if L>=3, because an L2 can't pay. The flat F+1 marked every tile in swarms.
- The queen died walking blind through a portal (default s1 r65): PORTAL_BLIND_PENALTY is 0 for small dragons. Added QUEEN_BLIND_PEN=200 and QUEEN_PEXIT_PEN=80.
- QUEEN_ALLY_THREAT is off. DEATHS.md shows 0 ally head-on queen deaths, and the rule pushed feeders away from the trauma pen-king queen.
- Map modules can still crown the queen. On trauma the queen IS the pen king (TRA_PENKING), and iAmKing() returns only that crown. She never self-crowns.
- EAT_SPRINT=0 and REPLY_SEARCH=0 in v35, so those parts of FREE_FIX are inert. FREE_FIX acts through tryKingEscape costs and FREE_SURVIVE.
