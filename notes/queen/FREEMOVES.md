# Free moves ceil(L/4) (unswbc 1.2.3): v35 audit + plan, 2026-10-01
Rule (from the helper.hpp/helper.py docs; I did not test it): a length-L dragon gets F=ceil(L/4) free steps, then pays 1 segment per step and must keep >=2 segments. Max reach is F+L-2 (old: L-1). F is 1 for L<=4, 2 for L 5-8, 3 for L 9-12, 4 for L 13-16, 5 for L 17-20. Still to check in one local game: is F fixed at the turn's starting L, and does a free step onto a pearl give +1 (a paid one is net 0, as before)?
## 1. Threat reach (computeThreats l.2287, replyEnemyReach l.3191; params l.57/67/565/586)
- computeThreats reach = min(max(1, visLen-1), cap). The cap is MAX_THREAT_REACH=1 when myLen<8 and KING_THREAT_REACH=4 when myLen>=8.
- Wrong #1, small dragons: they model only 1-step rams, but every enemy with L>=5 can now ram from 2 tiles for free (L>=9 from 3 tiles). SMALL_SPRINT_THREAT (2-step, l.2289) exists but is 0, and it would also skip all our dragons with myLen>=8.
- Wrong #2, king/queen cap of 4: an enemy with L>=13 rams for free from 4 tiles, and one with L>=17 from 5, which the cap ignores entirely. A 5-6 step ram by an L=12 enemy now costs 2-3 segments instead of 4-5.
- Wrong #3, visLen-1: this is the old cost model. The reach that actually matters is F (free), plus 1-2 paid steps when the target is worth it. visLen is clipped by the 7x7 view, so it understates F for big dragons. THREAT_TAIL_UNKNOWN=2 is now too low; use >= F(beacon or max seen length).
- replyEnemyReach has the same formula and cap 4. The reply search (l.3215, REPLY_RADIUS=3) enumerates only 1-step enemy replies, but a real reply is up to F steps, so we miss lines where an enemy closes 2-3 tiles.
- freeOther/freeT (l.1044) assume enemy tails move 1 tile per turn. A free sprint moves them up to F. That is conservative for us, so it is fine.
## 2. Our multi-step moves (all assume cost n-1)
- tryEatSprint l.2879: net=eaten-(steps-1) should be eaten-max(0,steps-F). tailFreed=(steps-eaten)+max(0,steps-1) should be (steps-eaten)+max(0,steps-F). v35 OVERCOUNTS freed tail, so a 3-step sprint with L>=5 can step onto its own tail, which has not left yet: a self-kill. maxSteps=min(3, L-1) and "pearls only after step 1" also waste free steps.
- tryRam l.2960: a 2-step ram (d1 to d2) is free for L>=5. Rams stop at distance 2, but L>=9 could ram for free from 3. val ignores cost, so only the reach needs widening.
- tryKingEscape l.3337: n=2..3 only, and only when every 1-step move is threatened. Penalty ESC_SPRINT_COST*(n-1)=15/step should be 15*max(0,n-F). Its tailFreed has the same overcount: +1 per k>0 should be +1 only per k>=F. The guard myLen-(n-1)<2 and area<myLen-(n-1) should use n-F.
- Single-step paths that ignore free steps: emitMove l.1406, the reverse moves at l.2266/l.3651, and the catch fallback at l.4261. Correct, but they waste F-1 free steps every turn, so a queen L>=9 moves at a third of her possible speed.
- surviveDepth l.1224: depth counts steps and treats a step as one turn (freeOther[v] <= t). Once we move F steps per turn, gate it on turn = ceil(t/F) or it will think enemy bodies clear too early.
## 3. Opportunities and changes (budget 100M; v35 p50 6.6M, max 14.4M)
- A. FREE_PATH (for L>=5; the queen is the main case). Enumerate simple paths of length 1..F with the body simulated (tail release 1/step, 0 on an eat). Path count: 4,12,36,108,324, so <=484 for F=5. Cheap pass: pearls eaten, threatFree/threatPaid at the endpoint, pearl-BFS distance, exits. About 200 ops/path, so <0.2M. Dedupe by (endpoint, eaten). Then run the full v35 eval (bfsFrom+surviveDepth+reply) on the top K=6. At ~1.5-3M each that is 10-20M, for a total of ~25-35M. Hard stop at pointsM()>60M with fallback to the best 1-step.
- Score a multi-step path only when it beats the best 1-step by a margin. Stopping short of F is free and keeps options open. Gains: up to F pearls/turn on a cluster, and pearl races won at F tiles/turn.
- B. Two-tier threat map: threatFree = BFS to F(Le) (penalty as now), threatPaid = F+1..F+2 (about 1/3 penalty, only for enemy Le>=5). The queen keeps its head beyond max F_enemy+1. Cost <0.1M (<=61 tiles per head).
- C. KING/QUEEN escape: run it proactively (not only when every step is threatened). Search n<=F+1 with tail-correct legality, score = dist to nearest enemy-free-reach + area. Reuse A's enumeration: 0 extra.
- D. tryRam: allow d<=F for L>=5, with no length cost. Require landing outside the other heads' threatFree.
- E. Port v36q's tailFreed/net fixes to v35 now (a self-kill bug fix, low risk), behind a FREE_COST_FIX flag (additive rule). Then A+B behind FREE_PATH and THREAT_FREE flags, and A/B them vs v35 on 6 maps x 3 seeds.
