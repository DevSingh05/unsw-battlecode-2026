# v36b: phased queen and queen-as-king (unswbc 1.2.3), 2026-10-01
New flags in variants/v36/params.hpp. The defaults keep the V36.md behaviour: QS_FROM=0, QS_UNITS=0, QS_EARLY_RAM=1, QUEEN_KING=0.
- QS_FROM / QS_UNITS: QUEEN_SAFE switches on at round QS_FROM, or once units >= QS_UNITS. It stays on after that.
  Before that the queen plays v35 with three limits:
  - She rams only strictly shorter heads, and only if QS_EARLY_RAM=1.
  - A tile next to an enemy head of length >= hers costs -300.
  - She keeps >= 3 when splitting (no PF or king-escape reverse splits).
- QUEEN_KING: on kingDeclare maps, from RP.kingSeedRound our queen is the only king. Non-queens don't crown while they have heard or seen her within QUEEN_ALIVE_MEM=30 rounds. They follow, feed and relay her (beacon tracking ported from v36q).
Batch: default, schooltime, devil, trauma, gen/g2026_01, gen/g2026_02, genhold/g2027_00. Seeds 1-3, both sides, 42 games vs v35, --jobs 2.

## Findings
- Phasing restores v35's win rate (~.50) but brings v35's queen deaths back. In QS_FROM=150, 23 of 37 queen deaths were head-on before r100, which is when the early queen plays like v35.
  Banning her early rams changes nothing (b100n: .14 alive). She dies from being rammed in v35 movement, not from her own rams.
- So early survival needs the full avoidance from r0. That costs the early brawls: 11-29 in eliminations for QS_FROM=0 vs ~9-10 for the phased variants.
  42 games give +-15% at 95%, though, and S1+S2+S3 alone scored .50 at seeds 1-3 and .36 at seeds 4-6.
- QUEEN_KING hurts schooltime (0/6). The crowned queen grows (28 by r350 in a smoke game), then coils into herself (HitSelf r230-399), and our longest falls behind.
  On trauma it doesn't close the length gap either. Seed 1 smoke game: ours 21 vs v35's 34 at r400, then a trap reverse split leaves her at 2 at ~r450.
  The trauma module already crowns her (TRA_PENKING), so the gap comes from her growth, not from who is king. S1's head/exit scoring slows her pen pumping.
- Not tried: S4 lookahead (PLAN.md). A big king queen needs it most: the anti-coil queenOpen() and the 2-3 ply reply check.

## Recommendation
Keep the defaults as S1+S2+S3 with QS_FROM=0 and QUEEN_KING=0: best survival, win .43 vs v35. Don't ship on these numbers.
Next: S4 anti-coil and lookahead for the queen. Then QUEEN_KING only on trauma (or with QUEEN_ENCLOSE), and a larger 84+ game sample per config before deciding.
