# v36c: S4 queen lookahead (unswbc 1.2.3), 2026-10-01
New flags in variants/v36/params.hpp, all off by default (v36 stays at S1+S2+S3 with QS_FROM=0):
- QUEEN_LOOK=1: computeQueenLook() runs one BFS per visible enemy head (bodies that stay put block it) out to 2F(L) (tail unseen: L>=4).
  A tile is unsafe after the worst reply if some head reaches it with F free steps now plus F next turn.
  Each queen candidate pays QL_EXIT_PEN=80 for every safe exit short of 2.
- QUEEN_ENCLOSE_PEN=400: v36q's queenOpen() (her body as a wall). Pays 400 x the missing fraction when the open area is below 3L+20.
- QUEEN_DIST_VETO=1: veto a step that ends within F(L)+1 (Manhattan) of any visible enemy head, as long as some step is far enough, unthreatened and >= 4 deep.
Compute (--sandbox, trauma s2, all on): p50 4.7M, max 8.6M points per turn (limit 40M).
Batch: default, schooltime, devil, trauma, gen/g2026_01, gen/g2026_02, genhold/g2027_00. Seeds 1-6, both sides: 84 games vs v35, --jobs 2.

## Where the losses are
Elimination W-L for every 42-game batch since V36.md:
- Queen flags off: 10-10. S3 only: 11-10. S2 only: 9-13.
- S1 variants: 8-12, 7-16, 7-14, 8-14. S1+S2: 5-14, 7-14.
- S1 games that reach r500 stay about even or better (S1+S2+S3: 25-19).
So the whole deficit is eliminations, and it appears as soon as the queen is passive (S1). Lookahead can't fix that: it makes her more passive.
v35 at r0: the queen splits and rams like any unit, so one passive unit in 3-5 early units is a large share of the early brawl.
A second cost is possible on default and the gen maps: a queen whose head is "threatened" never splits (`!threatened`), so the early unit count may be lower. Not measured yet.
Genhold 0/12 alive in every config: she dies in L2-3 swarms at r28-137 whatever she does.

## Suggested next steps (not done)
1. Measure the unit count at r20/r50 for S1 vs v35, and drop the queen's "threatened, no split" guard in the first ~20 rounds.
2. An escort: the nearest 1-2 allies ram anything within 2 of the queen (DEATHS.md "bodyguard"). This puts back the fighting value the passive queen loses.
3. Test vs bots whose queens die early (the server meta). There a surviving queen wins every r500 game, which v35 self-play undervalues.
