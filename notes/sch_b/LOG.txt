Design:
 - 4 clusters (team-A coords, team B mirrors x): 0 our west (8,2)(9,5)(10,7); 1 our east (14,3)(14,5)(20,4);
   2 their east (45,3)(45,5)(39,4); 3 their west (51,2)(50,5)(49,7). SCH_B_KEEP_CL mask, default 3 (ours).
   Each cluster fits a 7x7 view (park at (9,4) / (17,4)) so a keeper sees all its countdowns.
 - Decided once per process at birth (children are fresh processes), on layout B only, myLen<=LEN:
   nearest cluster in mask within RANGE steps -> keeper, unless a lower-id ally head is already in that zone
   (parent / starter twin). Result at r0: 1 west keeper (3,2), 1-2 east keepers (16,3)/(23,3) -> resolves to 1.
 - Each turn: outside zone (>ZONE steps) pull PULL/step back; inside, -LEAVE for stepping out; +TW/step toward
   the cluster tile with least max(dist, countdown) (countdown from w.spawnAt / pearlMem / lastSeen+20).
 - Yield: a lower-id ally head in our zone for YIELD consecutive turns -> released for good.
 - Suspended while myLen>LEN or kingSelf; released at round R. A keeper's split child is not a keeper (it sees the
   parent), so the keeper acts as a pump into the maze.
 Defaults: R 400, LEN 6, RANGE 6, ZONE 4, PULL 3.0, LEAVE 12, TW 1.5, YIELD 3.
