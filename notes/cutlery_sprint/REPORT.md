## Cross-cutting mechanics
- **Splitting.** Almost all early splits are L4 to 2+2. Splits before r100: Aus 71 vs our 25 (similar elsewhere). The gap is food and survival, not split rate.
- **Tail-flip.** They do 57 per big-map game, against 15 for opponents and 6 for us (2040 over 36 games). It is a split where L≥8 and k≥L-3: the head keeps 2 segments and the long body reverses out through its tail. 76% happen with 0-1 free exits at the head, so it is an escape from a dead end or corridor. 57% of the children live another 20+ rounds. This is also why their final king's id is only fixed at r350-480. Example: on Maze 806970 they flipped a 69 into a 67 at r485.
- **Suicides.** An invalid `split:1` gives a NoValidAction death: 150-585 per game, 97% of them L2-3. 40-60% happen in 3-kelp dead ends, and most are ≥9 from the king. This is stuck-unit recycling, not king feeding.
