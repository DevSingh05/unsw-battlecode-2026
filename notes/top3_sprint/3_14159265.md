   lose because our unit count stops growing between r50 and r100 (6 to 1, 6 to 5), not because we lose trades.

   the Devil and Trophy losses we sent 0 and 1 pings. Rays report kelp, ally, allyHead and enemy hits, so this is almost certainly mapping and pearl or
   threat sharing. It would explain their larger visited area.
C. **Deliberate recycle deaths of length-2/3 units into kelp ("HitWall") next to an ally.** 767 of 920 on Slithery and 59 of 59 on Devil had an
   ally within 3, and the ally eats the corpse (own-corpse eats 91 on Devil, 1298 on Slithery). We do the same thing, but as HitSelf, and in
   similar volume on big maps (950 on Slithery). This one is not a gap.
D. Their mid-sized splits (L=5/6 into k=3/4) show up on open maps: Slithery k3=231 and k4=219, against 32 and 6 for us. Our children are almost always 2.
